Questions

How long does it take?

You'll get your listings within a day. Priority requests go to the front of the line for same-day results.

What does the fee cover?

The flat fee pays for the search and for checking each listing. The price of the item itself is not included. You buy it directly from the seller on Amazon, eBay, KSL or Facebook Marketplace.

How do I pay?

With Venmo. After you fill in the form you'll see the amount, a QR code and a link. Add your reference code in the Venmo note so we can match your payment, then tap "I've paid."

How will I receive my listings?

By email or text, whichever you give us in the form. Each message includes direct links, prices and short notes on each listing.

What if you can't find a good match?

We'll message you with what we tried and what your options are, like adjusting the budget, condition or specs.

Is my information safe?

We only use your name and contact details to deliver your listings. We don't sell or share them.
